Questions

Good to know.

Who is SchoolBook built for?

Principal, teacher, counselor, bursar, librarian, student, and guardian accounts. Each one signs in to the same workspace and sees the slice of it that matches their responsibilities.

Can more than one school use it?

Yes. Each school is a separate tenant with its own roster, ledger, and asset register. A signed-in account only sees the school it belongs to; there is no cross-school data sharing.

What does the student portal show?

Subject-level scores with teacher comments, attendance history, the current standing indicator, official course documents, and any fees still outstanding.

How do guardians receive notices?

A circular is written once and targeted to the campus, a department, or a single cohort. When the deployment has an email provider configured, guardian email delivery is queued from the same record; the feed shows the intended recipient count.

How are fees tracked?

Tuition, lab and library fees, extracurricular charges, and donations share one ledger. The dashboard projects weekly collection pace so a term can be forecast before it ends.

Does anything need to be installed?

No. SchoolBook runs in the browser. Sign in with the account your school administrator created. There is nothing to install on staff, student, or family devices.
